# Fusion Validation Limitations

## What This Validates
- Behavioral response of the fusion engine to different modality configurations
- Uncertainty increases when modalities are missing or low quality
- Safety recommendations remain conservative
- Modality ablation effects on proxy outputs

## What This Does NOT Validate
- Accuracy against ground-truth cognitive states
- Clinical inference or diagnosis
- Thought or dream decoding
- Physical hardware multimodal sensor integration
- Real EEG closed-loop control

## Scientific Disclaimers
- Multimodal fusion outputs are experimental proxy estimates, not clinical or unrestricted mental-state measurements.
- The corridor is not a decoded mental image. It is an adaptive scaffold driven by experimental proxy metrics.
- Closed-loop from real EEG is disabled by default.
- IMAGINA plugin outputs are experimental imagery-quality proxies, not decoded thoughts or dreams.
- Fusion validation is behavioral testing under simulated conditions. It does not prove accuracy against ground-truth cognitive states.
